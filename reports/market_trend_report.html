How competitors are scored

Every competitor gets a similarity score (0–1) against the imaginary anchor — a weighted mix of four components (weights sum to 1.0):
ComponentWeightRule
📍 Location35% Haversine distance from the anchor: 0 km = 1.0, falling linearly to 0.0 at 20 km
🛏 Bedrooms30% Exact 4BR match = 1.0; −0.25 per bedroom of difference
🏠 Property type30% Exact match = 1.0; same broad category (house / townhouse / cabin / …) = 0.7; different category = 0
💰 Price5% Log-ratio to the anchor's $250: within ±20% → >0.8; 2× away → ≈0.25
This report shows only competitors scoring ≥ 0.70 — weak matches are excluded. Scoring lives in market_agent/competitor_scorer.py.

How the recommended price is calculated

  1. Comp set — the top 20 closest competitors by similarity score (needs ≥ 4 priced comps). The daily pipeline ranks the full scored set, independent of this report's ≥ 0.70 display cutoff.
  2. Anchor — the median effective (post-discount) nightly price of that comp set.
  3. Quality multiplier (×0.90–1.15) — rating vs comp median (±0.5★ ≈ ±10%), +3% Guest Favorite, +2% Superhost. Quality multipliers apply only when rating/badges are set on the anchor profile — without them it stays ×1.00.
  4. Demand multiplier (×0.90–1.10) — >50% of comps discounting → −6%; >30% → −3%; fewer than 30% of comps still available → +3%.
  5. Clamp & round — anchor × quality × demand, clamped to the comp-set interquartile range (no suggestion outside what the market bears), rounded to $5.
